## Which parts are intent

The intent: no data loss anywhere, ever; machinery that makes losing data impossible even for someone who does not know what they are doing, the way git itself does; a recovery cache under every step that overwrites, changes or deletes files, without leaning on it; and behaviour that stays as close as possible to git's own, so that git's tooling, merge drivers and LFS work unchanged. The rest is kept for provenance. His question about a parallel branch path is a question, not a decision: "I'm not saying do that, I'm just asking".

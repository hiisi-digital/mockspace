The matcher at tree.rs:277-289 backtracks without memoisation.
The cost is COMBINATORIAL in (stars k, component length n), not
simply exponential in k: the ratios above flatten past k = n/2
because the number of ways to place k cut points in n characters
peaks there and then falls. An earlier run of this probe with
k = 16 against n = 40, which is much nearer that peak, had not
returned after roughly 400 seconds and was killed. That is an
existence claim about non-termination in practice; the table
above is a shape, and neither is a price.
Patterns are consumer-authored in `[benchspace] members` / `exclude`
and are matched against every discovered path (tree.rs:197, :261),
so this is a hazard rather than a live defect: the realistic
patterns (`**`, `bench-*`, `*-probe`) sit far from the wall.

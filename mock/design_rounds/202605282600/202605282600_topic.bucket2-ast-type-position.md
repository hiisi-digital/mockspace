# Why this round exists

The in-process `ast-type-position` builtin (`mockspace-rs/src/builtins/ast_type_position.rs`) flags forbidden type names at type-bearing positions (struct fields, enum-variant fields, fn params, fn returns, type-alias bodies), gated by visibility. It backs eight catalogue presets: no-public-raw-field, no-bare-numeric, no-bare-option, no-bare-result, no-bare-string, no-vec-in-trait-sig, strategy-marker-required, trait-first-signatures. Porting it onto the cdylib boundary makes those presets runnable through the viola host.

This is the highest-value remaining bucket-2 port. The sibling primitive `ast-node-position-match` is deferred: it ships no catalogue preset of its own (its doc-comment example lints, no-todo / no-runtime-spawn / no-dyn-dispatch, already ship as token-scan, ported in bucket-1), so a cdylib port has no preset consumer yet.

# What lands

A new `extern "C"` evaluator `ast_type_position_evaluate` behind `PROVIDER_AST_TYPE_POSITION` (`mockspace-builtin.lint.ast-type-position.v2`), registered in the `#[export_extension]` providers list as the eighth provider. Supporting decode + walk helpers and eleven unit tests against synthetic `NamNode` arrays.

# Scope boundary: position coverage

The v1.2.0 node-kind table expresses three of the five type positions: StructField (FIELD_DECLARATION 22), EnumVariantField (FIELD_DECLARATION under ENUM_VARIANT 24, or tuple-style types directly under ENUM_VARIANT), and FnParam (PARAMETER 23). FnReturn and TypeAliasBody have no dedicated position-kind node in the v1.2.0 table, so they are out of scope this slice and await a return-type / type-alias-body position kind in a later NAM bump. Documented at the evaluator.

# Doc changelist: the bench consolidation round

**Date:** 2026-08-15
**Phase:** DOC
**Topics:** `202608151338` (validation), `202608151339` (configuration), `202608151340` (the record)
**Source material:** six research files on this branch, two investigations, three proposals and one
cross-check

## What this round decided

Three subjects were designed independently and then audited at their seams. The audit found no
contradiction between them and seven gaps at the boundaries, all of which it resolved. Its findings
and the settled decisions are below. Nothing here is implemented; the source changelists follow.

## The finding that reordered everything

**PR #21 is open on the identical surfaces and nobody designed against it, because the coordinator
did not name it in any brief.** It already implements two-level identity with the composite key,
nested artifact naming, a generated driver, four hooks with a failing verdict withholding the
history append, and build overrides.

Two proposals therefore carry checkably false claims about it, and the record proposal's most
careful argument is reasoned correctly from a surface that PR obsoletes. The round's own failure was
the arc's failure: believing by reading rather than running. The instruments were sound and were
pointed at one branch too few.

**Consequence: PR #21 lands first.** Holding it stales nine commits of mechanism this round
endorses; folding it in makes one unreviewable change; discarding it rebuilds identity, hooks,
generation and withholding from prose written by looking at it.

## Settled by op, not reopened here

Membership is declared rather than detected, `[benchspace] members` defaulting to `["**"]`. The
per-file bench form has no wrapper table, its fields top-level. Explicit `[bench.<name>]` keying
stays supported indefinitely; nothing is deprecated. Outputs live in one shared tree. The metadata
base is one `CellRecord` with every other writer a projection. All four hooks ship, including the
two with no current caller, recorded as op's call for lifecycle completeness rather than as an
evidenced need.

## The change sequence

**Change 1, one source changelist, indivisible.** Verdict semantics and the record together: the
`validate()` return-shape change carrying per-arm structural failures and per-pair mismatches; the
driver consuming it, with structural drops and mismatch tainting both unconditional; the reps-1
validation-mode forcing in `bench-core`; `CellRecord` written in the post-cell block, including for
cells that fail before measuring; the digest's post-cell comparison, which makes it load-bearing for
the first time; the `resolve_routine` policy cross-check that makes `may_differ` meaningful on the
hook path; the projection policy with `schema_v2`'s appended columns and a fixture test for the
untested history read path; the profile handoff and the deletion of the hardcoded literal; the false
doc comment at `config.rs:120-122`; and the deletion of the tautology at `bench-core/src/lib.rs:637`,
which nothing else in flight performs.

**These are one change because every split leaves a lie standing.** Semantics without storage is
stderr again. Storage without semantics is an empty field. Tainting without reps-1 is a flake
generator. A record without the projection policy launders the ledger.

**Change 2, separable, framework-side sweep support.** The per-file `[axis.*]` and `[sweep.*]` schema
with `points` and `hold`, refusing an undeclared held axis; the point rendering rule, injective by
construction and degenerating to today's `_n<value>`; the index-over-FFI dispatch with the abi-hash
version fold **as one atom**, because the split state is silently wrong for four of sixteen indices
in one consumer; the generator gaining the four policy keys; the cell-to-arm rename in
`bench-matrix`; and the supersession of `routine_table!`.

**Change 2 leaves no trap if it never lands**, which is why it is separable: change 1's record holds
a single-axis map, filenames stay at the degenerate rendering, and the pseudo-arm category error gets
honestly tainted with a one-line consumer remedy available.

**Then the consumer migrations**, per repo and on their own rounds. Not this repository's change, and
the abi version fold is what makes that phase boundary safe.

## Cut

The manifest-layer hard refusal requiring explicit policy on every multi-arm bench. With tainting and
the record, silence is no longer silent, which was the refusal's entire justification, and it
interacts incoherently with the dead-key path. The honest kernel of it survives in the
`resolve_routine` cross-check.

## Deferred, with successors owed

Digest computation extended to hand-written arms. The `Routine`-contract explicit declaration. Wiring
the cache's skip-rerun system into the driver. End-to-end wiring of the bootstrap iteration count,
where the record carries the resolved value and honouring it is a pre-existing task.

## Not cut despite temptation

Records for cells that fail before measuring, which the topic ratified and whose reordering cost was
priced honestly. And the four generator policy keys, which look like change-2 sugar and are in fact
the only path by which 135 of 180 sections in the largest consumer ever acquire a validation policy
at all.

## Not changed

The measurement core entire: the scaffold seed table, the split between setup and measured work, the
anti-hoist, the calibration floor. The per-variant cdylib isolation. The shared realistic workload.
The committed artifact trail. The one-const-parameter rule, which is preserved by giving that
parameter a struct type rather than by lifting it. And the samples CSV's columns.

## Open, and op's

Whether the Cargo manifest version moves. mockspace sits at `0.1.0` with no tags at all, and the
release cadence names `0.0.0-dNN` for pre-release markers, so the two schemes currently disagree.
This round would land as `d01`, the repository's first.

## Lock criteria

This changelist locks when PR #21 has merged and the source changelist for change 1 exists. It does
not lock before, because the identity shape change 1 records is the shape that PR defines.
